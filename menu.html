Around the World
in 12 Desserts

Every dessert is made to order. Pick your dish, pick your date, and give us 48 hours to do it justice.

All prices are per single portion unless noted. Full cakes and platters are available — ask Dr. Hetanshi. Seasonal availability may affect some dishes. All ingredients are sourced fresh.

Europe

Dessert Origin Description Price
Tiramisu
🇮🇹 Italy Espresso-soaked ladyfingers layered with silken mascarpone, finished with a generous dusting of high-quality cocoa. Cloud-soft and unapologetically rich. ₹280
Baklava
🇬🇷 Greece Thirty paper-thin filo layers, crushed pistachios, and rose-infused honey syrup. Sweet, floral, and addictive. Served in a portion of four pieces. ₹260
Crème Caramel (Flan)
🇫🇷 France Silky baked custard, unmoulded over a pool of glossy amber caramel. Simple perfection that has stood the test of centuries. ₹230
Churros
🇪🇸 Spain Golden, ridged dough fried to order and rolled in cinnamon sugar, served with a pot of deep, dark chocolate dipping sauce. Best eaten immediately. ₹200
Medovik (Honey Cake)
🇷🇺 Russia Multiple whisper-thin honey sponge layers separated by tangy sour cream frosting, rested overnight until impossibly tender. A 36-hour labour of love. ₹350 / slice · ₹1,800 whole
* All European desserts use imported mascarpone and Belgian chocolate where specified.

Mochi
🇯🇵 Japan Soft, pillowy glutinous rice cakes with a sweet red bean or matcha filling. Served in a set of three. Chewy, subtle, and utterly satisfying. ₹220
Kulfi Falooda
